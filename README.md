> **High-Performance Motorcycle Architecture &bull; Aerodynamic Class-A Surfacing &bull; 1:1 Physical Prototyping**

A portfolio website crafted specifically for **Aryan Pevekar**, Transportation &amp; Motorcycle Designer. Designed in a high-contrast **Obsidian Black and Electric Cyan (`#00E5FF` / `#0284c7`)** automotive aesthetic with full **Light Studio Mode** support.

## 🏍️ Flagship Projects Showcased

### 1. APEX Bespoke Hyper-Streetfighter Concept (MV Agusta Inspired)
- **Category:** Concept Motorcycle Architecture &bull; F1 Downforce Aerodynamics
- **Design DNA:** Sculpted Surface Tension, Sidepod Downforce Channels, Cantilevered Venturi Tail.
- **Interactive Features:**
  - **6-Camera Viewport Switcher:** Front 3/4 Dynamic, Direct Front, Top-Down Bird's Eye, Charcoal Study, Studio Profile (Dark), Studio Profile (Gold).
  - **Before/After Comparison Slider:** Raw Pencil Proportion Architecture vs. Photorealistic Obsidian Dark Studio Render.
  - **Comprehensive 3-Phase Gallery:** Presentation boards 12..24, F1 aerodynamic research, moodboards, ideation sketches, Class-A surfacing, and environmental lighting renders.

### 2. Aprilia Tuono 457 &mdash; Modular Quick-Attach Luggage Architecture
- **Category:** Ergonomic Utility &amp; Motorcycle Accessory Design
- **Problem Solved:** Commuter backpack shoulder fatigue and heat buildup on sports motorcycles.
- **Innovation:** Tool-free 3-second slide-in latch locking onto OEM pillion mounts without bodywork alteration.
- **Physical Validation:** Full 1:1 scale FDM 3D printed prototype, hand-finished white surfacing model, and live on-motorcycle outdoor rigidity testing.
- **Interactive Features:**
  - **6-Angle Component Switcher:** Bag Loadout, CNC Rack Component, Dual-Material Frame, Latch Tolerances, Live Bike Test, Factory Color Match.
  - **11-Stage Ideation Evolution:** High-contrast studio sketch sheets.
  - **Official Presentation Poster Download:** Full-scale 300 DPI PDF (21.8 MB).

### 3. Custom JAWA Bobber Motorcycle Architecture
- **Category:** Modern Classic Cruiser &bull; Custom Surfacing
- **Design DNA:** Low-Slung Cruiser Stance, Teardrop Tank Surfacing, Cantilever Solo Saddle, Mechanical Purity.
- **Interactive Features:**
  - **6-Angle Viewport Switcher:** Dynamic 3/4 Hero, Side Profile Studio, Front Low-Angle, Tank Surfacing, Rear Floating Tail, Complete 3D Wireframe Mesh.
  - **Before/After Comparison Slider:** Selected Ideation Line Drawing vs. Dynamic Photorealistic Studio Render.
  - **Complete 16-Board Presentation:** All 16 boards (Slides 25..40) + 5 high-resolution CAD digital modeling screenshots (Engine bay, Tank surface, Cantilever saddle, Wheel hub, Wireframe mesh).

## ⚡ Key Interactive Features
1. **Dark & Light Mode Switcher:** Seamless toggle between Obsidian Carbon and Daylight Studio modes with anti-flicker pre-initialization.
2. **3 Multi-Angle Interactive Viewports:** Smooth cross-fade vehicle rotation.
3. **2 Sketch-to-Render Comparison Sliders:** Draggable before/after inspection for Apex and Jawa Bobber.
4. **Fullscreen Lightbox with Dark/Light Canvas Toggle:** Zoom and inspect all 94 assets with keyboard arrows (`ArrowLeft`, `ArrowRight`, `Escape`).
5. **Real Resume Integration:** Instant preview and one-click download of Aryan's verified resume (`Resume/Resume.pdf`).
6. **Zero Dependencies:** Pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript. Runs 100% offline.
