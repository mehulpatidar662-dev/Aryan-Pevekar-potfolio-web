APEX Bespoke Hyper-Streetfighter Concept

Flagship Concept F1 Downforce Shrouds Trellis Monocoque

A track-bred bespoke hyper-streetfighter motorcycle engineered to bridge aerodynamic downforce technology from modern Formula 1 with visceral, knife-edge two-wheeler proportions. Developed through rapid pencil architecture, biomorphic airflow ideation, Class-A CAD digital surfacing, and multi-light environment studio renderings.

01
Sculpted Surface Tension

Razor-sharp character lines contrasting seamless fluid transitions across the fuel tank, radiator cowls, and chassis shoulders.

02
F1 Vortex Ground Effect

Sidepod-inspired negative-pressure air ducts channeling airflow around the rider's knees directly to high-capacity cooling radiators.

03
Cantilevered Venturi Tail

Minimalist structural rear subframe floating above an inverted diffuser tunnel to minimize aerodynamic drag at triple-digit speeds.

02 // AUTOMOTIVE ACCESSORY • ERGONOMIC UTILITY

Aprilia Tuono 457 — Modular Quick-Attach Luggage Architecture

1:1 Validated Prototype Slide-In Quick Latch FDM 3D Printed

An industrial accessory architecture project solving rider backpack fatigue, shoulder strain, and thermal buildup on aggressive sport motorcycles. Engineered with a slide-in structural side-rack that locks directly into OEM Aprilia Tuono 457 pillion grab mounts without tools, drilling, or disturbing the bike's aggressive tail silhouette.

01
3-Second Slide Latch

Tool-free quick-attach slide mechanism engaging internal subframe ribs with a spring-loaded positive retention detent.

02
Subframe Topology Fitment

Modeled over high-precision 3D scan data of the Aprilia Tuono 457 rear subframe to maintain 100% factory bodywork clearance.

03
1:1 Full-Scale Prototype

Validated through full-scale functional FDM 3D printing, surface primer finishing, and live outdoor load testing on an actual motorcycle.

Custom JAWA Bobber Motorcycle Architecture

Complete Design Architecture Teardrop Tank Surfacing Cantilever Solo Saddle

Explores low-slung cruiser stance, sculpted teardrop tank surfacing, minimalist floating cantilevered solo saddle, bespoke exhaust header routing, and production-feasible chassis packaging presented through comprehensive design research and detailed 3D CAD modeling.

Fluid Class-A sculpted fuel tank featuring subtle knee recesses and a seamless junction into the floating cantilever solo saddle.

03
Minimal Mechanical Packaging

Tightly routed twin exhaust headers, hidden mono-shock suspension linkage, and exposed engine cylinder fins highlighting mechanical purity.

Design Methodology

Design Methodology

As a hearing-impaired transportation designer, I navigate vehicle design through heightened visual intuition, tactile observation, and spatial awareness. Every surface is sculpted as a purposeful synthesis of mechanical packaging, rider ergonomics, and emotional impact.

01

Ergonomic Architecture & Hardpoints

Establishing the primary mechanical package: engine mount geometry, rake & trail steering angles, rider triangle (hip, knee, footpeg, handlebar), and suspension travel arcs before surface styling commences.

02

Analogue Ideation & Character Curves

Rapid paper and digital sketching to define dynamic gesture speedlines. Testing visual mass concentration, front-to-rear stance bias, and light-reflecting shoulder highlights.

03

3D Digital Modeling & CFD Surfacing

Translating 2D curves into high-precision 3D polygon and NURBS Class-A surfaces. Simulating aerodynamic downforce, airflow channel clearances, and tooling parting lines.

04

Physical 1:1 Validation & Prototyping

Bringing digital CAD into physical reality via full-scale 3D printing, hand sanding, metal sculpting, and on-motorcycle fitment tests to validate real-world rigidity and emotional presence.

About the Designer

Form in Service of Speed, Aerodynamics & Utility

I am a Transportation Design student at MIT ADT University, Pune driven by a deep fascination with motorcycle dynamics, aerodynamic efficiency, and visceral mechanical tension.

As a hearing-impaired designer, my perception of the world is inherently visual, spatial, and tactile. This gives me a unique advantage in industrial design: I perceive proportion nuances, surface highlight curvature, and mechanical clearances with uncompromising precision.

Whether developing Formula 1-inspired aerodynamic downforce wings for a hyperbike, solving commuter ergonomic pain points with 1:1 validated 3D-printed accessory prototypes, or sculpting vintage cruiser curves on the Jawa Bobber, I approach every project with rigorous manufacturing feasibility and intense aesthetic passion.
